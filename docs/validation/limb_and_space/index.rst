=============================================
Limb, Occultation, and Satellite-to-Satellite
=============================================

These notebooks test the limb sounding, radio occultation, and satellite-to-satellite analyses against Aura MLS and TIMED SABER, the occultations of COSMIC-2 and PlanetiQ, the precise orbits of GRACE-FO, and the close approaches predicted by SOCRATES. The element sets they read are in the ``elements`` folder beside them. Their headline results are collected in the :doc:`../summary`.
