========================
Instruments and Pointing
========================

These notebooks test the models of scanners, pushbrooms, conical scanners, synthetic aperture radars, altimeters, and lidars, and of the pointing of their platforms, against the geolocated samples of operational instruments. Several derive the configurations of catalog entries and explain the modeling choices behind them; the entries' results on data held out from their fits are on the :doc:`../catalog` page. Their headline results are collected in the :doc:`../summary`.
