=================
Spacecraft Sizing
=================

These notebooks size spacecraft for their orbits and lives:

#. :doc:`SizeSpacecraft`: the density of the thermosphere and the solar
   cycle, the lifetimes of orbits under drag, the velocity budgets of orbits
   (holding them against drag and disposing of them within 25 years) and
   their propellant, sizing a notional spacecraft from its payload and a
   catalog spacecraft from its own resources, and the cost that sizing gives
   altitude in a trade study (which requires ``tatc[tradespace]``).
