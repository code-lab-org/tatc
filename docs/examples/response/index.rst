==========================
Event Response and Tasking
==========================

These notebooks measure how quickly concepts respond to events, and task
agile satellites to image them:

#. :doc:`RespondToEvents`: the responses of a wide-swath constellation and of
   an agile, narrow-field constellation tasked at two agilities to synthetic
   events over the western United States, from onset to first look and to
   delivery through KSAT's stations, and the responses of places to events at
   any time.
